# Vending Machine Design

## Introduction

A **vending machine** is a classic state-machine and OO design problem. It handles selection, payment, inventory, and dispensing.

## Core Entities

- **VendingMachine**: State (idle, selecting, payment, dispensing), inventory, cash.
- **Item**: Id, price, quantity.
- **Payment**: Cash or card; validate and complete.

## States and Transitions

- Idle to Selecting (user selects item).
- Selecting to Payment (user confirms; check stock).
- Payment to Dispensing (payment success); Dispensing to Idle (dispense and return change).

## Summary

- Model states clearly (state pattern or enum), validate inventory and payment, and handle change and errors.
