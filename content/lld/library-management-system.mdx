# Library Management System

## Introduction

A **library management system** models books, members, borrow/return, reservations, and fines. It is a good exercise in entities, relationships, and business rules.

## Core Entities

- **Book**: ISBN, title, copies, availability.
- **Member**: Id, name, borrowed items, reservations.
- **BorrowRecord**: Book, member, due date, returned date.
- **Reservation**: Book, member, queue position.

## Key Operations

- Borrow and return books (update availability and due dates).
- Reserve a book when all copies are borrowed.
- Calculate fines for late returns.
- Search and browse catalog.

## Summary

- Model books, members, borrow records, and reservations; enforce rules for max borrows, due dates, and fines.
