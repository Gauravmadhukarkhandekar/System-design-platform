# System Design Process

## Introduction

A structured process helps you go from a vague problem to a clear, discussable design. This is the typical flow used in interviews and in practice.

## Steps

1. **Clarify requirements** — Ask about scope, users, scale, and constraints. Define functional and non-functional requirements.
2. **Estimate scale** — Back-of-the-envelope: DAU/MAU, reads/writes per second, storage and bandwidth. This guides technology choices.
3. **High-level design** — Sketch the main components: clients, load balancers, app servers, databases, caches, queues. Draw a diagram.
4. **Deep dive** — Go into one or two critical areas (e.g., database schema, caching strategy, consistency model).
5. **Identify bottlenecks** — Consider single points of failure, scaling limits, and trade-offs (e.g., consistency vs availability).

## Summary

- Clarify, Estimate, High-level design, Deep dive, Bottlenecks.
- Revisit and refine as you get more constraints or feedback.
