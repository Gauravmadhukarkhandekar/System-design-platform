# Design Patterns Overview

## Introduction

**Design patterns** are reusable solutions to common problems in software design. They provide a shared vocabulary and proven structures for OO design.

## Categories

- **Creational**: How objects are created (Singleton, Factory, Builder).
- **Structural**: How objects are composed (Adapter, Decorator, Facade).
- **Behavioral**: How objects interact (Observer, Strategy, Command).

## Summary

- Use patterns to communicate and to structure code; avoid forcing them where they do not fit.
- Prefer clarity and simplicity over pattern-heavy design.
