# Design YouTube

## Introduction

A YouTube-like system handles video upload, processing, storage, streaming, and metadata (search, recommendations, comments). The design is write-heavy for uploads and read-heavy for streaming and discovery.

## Core Features

- Upload video; transcode and store; stream video; search and browse; recommendations; comments and likes; subscriptions.

## High-Level Design

- **Upload**: Client uploads to blob storage or via API; job queue for transcoding (multiple resolutions/formats); metadata stored in DB (title, description, owner, status).
- **Streaming**: Serve video from CDN; use HLS/DASH or similar for adaptive bitrate; CDN caches chunks by URL.
- **Metadata and search**: Videos, channels, playlists in DB; search index (e.g., Elasticsearch) for full-text and filters; recommendations from ML pipeline or collaborative filtering.
- **Comments and engagement**: Comments stored and paginated; likes/counts cached and eventually consistent with DB.
- **Subscriptions**: Graph of channel-to-subscribers; fan-out on new video (notification feed) or on read (fetch latest from subscribed channels).

## Summary

- Separate upload pipeline (blob + transcoding queue), streaming (CDN), metadata and search, and engagement. Scale upload and transcoding; optimize streaming with CDN; index and recommend for discovery.
